Questions

Got questions?

Who can participate in HackFusion 2026?
+

HackFusion is open to students, developers, designers, and AI enthusiasts across India. Beginners and experienced builders are welcome.

Is it a team or solo hackathon?
+

Teams of 2–4 are recommended, but solo participants can join and form teams through the community.

How much does it cost to register?
+

Registration is free for eligible participants. Event-specific benefits and requirements are shared with confirmed teams.

Who owns the work I build?
+

Teams retain ownership of their work, subject to the official rules and any third-party APIs, datasets, or services they use.

Do I need prior AI or machine learning experience?
+

No. The event is designed for different experience levels, with mentors and resources available throughout the build.

What is the prize pool?
+

The total prize pool is ₹1,00,000 in cash awards along with special category recognitions, free .xyz domain registration for every participant, certificates, and goodies.
